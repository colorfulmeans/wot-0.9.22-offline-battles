# wot-0.9.22-offline-battles v0.9.5

This is the cumulative changelog **since the published v0.9.4 release**. The release is built from the PR #40 line, including its subsequent repair branch, without incorporating the later abandoned PR branches. It supports the Chinese HD World of Tanks **0.9.22.0.1 #1513** client only.

## Main feature: visual Bot tactics editor

- Open **Tools → Bot tactics → Edit behavior, routes and artillery positions** in the launcher. The editor works with the installed game's original minimaps when available and shows the 41 supported maps with their actual bounds and team bases. A labelled navigation view is available when the minimap artwork cannot be loaded.
- Tune connected Bot gunnery controls for all Bots, a team, a vehicle class, or a specific team/slot: skill preset, crew level, reaction delay, aiming patience, convergence threshold, aim-point bias and lead error. More specific rules override inherited values. These controls do not directly change native damage, penetration, dispersion, spotting or reload.
- Draw attack routes on the map: copy a built-in route or create one, edit its waypoints and hold points, and select eligible classes, capacity, sampling weight and preferred or fixed route policy. The original navigator still checks reachability, terrain and obstacles; a fixed route keeps the planned lane while allowing necessary combat and emergency decisions.
- Mark artillery deployment areas with a centre, parking radius, initial heading and priority. The worker selects reachable, vehicle-sized parking within each area. Manually authored positions are available on all 41 maps. The supplied community-sourced position library covers **Ruinberg and Steppes** only; other maps continue to use the existing fallback until positions are authored.
- Keep multiple named profiles, import or export JSON, and save drafts independently of **Apply**. Applying validates and activates a profile for the host's **next battle**; it does not alter a battle already in progress. The host owns its settings when other players join, and profiles live outside the application and game folders.
- The map and built-in route names follow the launcher's **Auto / English / 中文** language selector, including editors already open. Switching languages preserves edits and map coordinates; user-created names stay as entered.

## Battle setup, artillery and Bot control

- Restore both `-2..0` and `0..+2` tier presets, choose one legal tier window containing the connected human vehicles, and mirror tier/class slots across the teams. Automatic lineups can include zero to three artillery vehicles per team, counting human artillery. Offline room sampling is not the retail queue algorithm.
- Repair the restored Bot's contact/impulse acknowledgement and radio-recipient filtering. Contacts received for a different Bot no longer grant targeting or movement knowledge; physical pushing uses the actual mass, engine-powered approach, track response and shared collision result. Keep the original v0.7.7 driving and traffic strategy and the 41 original navigation maps.
- Stop SPGs repeatedly turning toward a known target and then back to their base direction because an advisory firing proof expired. Keep real aim, native trajectory, cover, friendly-clearance, ammunition and reload checks. Use bounded proof refresh for moving targets, log actual firing gates, and permit a limited move to an existing safe waypoint after a nearby wall is confirmed to block all firing arcs.
- Add an initial SPG position library for **Ruinberg and Steppes** with source-labelled parking areas. Positions are chosen using the existing map grid, vehicle footprint and reachability checks; uncovered maps retain their old fallback. The library does not teleport vehicles or guarantee a firing solution.

## Ramming, wrecks and game feedback

- Repair high-speed collisions where momentum moved the vehicles but no HP was recorded because the probe missed one vehicle's structural armour. Sample matching native plates at a shared point inside the actual contact overlap. Preserve the existing collision mass, horizontal closing speed, contact armour and damage factors; do not retune the global damage scale. Regression coverage includes both mass directions and the hidden worker's contact path.
- Allow a wreck to keep moving across a narrow gap under its centre when both ends of its hull still have ground support. Repeated pushes from a player reach the worker, while duplicate delivered impulses are ignored. Cliffs, solid world barriers and vehicle-sized clearance remain checked. Wrecks do not take HP damage.
- Distinguish three HE outcomes in sound and crew feedback: nonpenetrating with no HP loss, nonpenetrating with blast damage, and penetrating with damage. Play the explosion for direct HE impacts as well as near misses.
- Make a retained player wreck block the reticle's ray when spectating after death, so a vehicle behind that wreck does not receive a false silhouette.
- Correct the **Mittengard** base circle to its verified 30 m radius. Read **Tundra** and **Live Oaks** team-specific circle radii from the installed original map data instead of guessing a shared radius.

## Garage, missions and supplies

- Feed human view ranges into Bot observations so the T28 Concept **HT-5** condition can evaluate damage within the actual view range. **LT-5 has no newly added implementation** in this release; its existing PR #40 conditions remain unchanged.
- Preserve the garage's selected shell-slot order in the battle gun and the authority worker's immutable gun contract.
- Relay all-target premium consumable activation even while a local vehicle model is temporarily unavailable; log equipment rejection reasons for diagnosis.
- Correct free-experience conversion with zero-debit vehicles so it does not falsely mark unowned vehicles as newly elite. Requalification resets crew skills through the native descriptor while preserving its accounting. Retain the existing exchange, crew and elite-notification corrections from the intervening builds.

## Installation and validation

Close the previous launcher and game. Extract the **entire** `wot-0.9.22-offline-battles-0.9.5-Windows-x64.zip` to a **new empty folder**, then run `wot-0.9.22-offline-battles.exe` and select your existing #1513 client. Do not merge old and new `_internal` directories. All LAN participants must use this matching package. Existing save slots do not require a reset.

The Windows build validates the server, Python 2.7 client payload, launcher, simulated #1513 installation and bundled-server startup. Focused collision and battle suites pass. The general Tests workflow still has a Bot gameplay-follow-up failure also present on the untouched PR #40 baseline. Neither CI nor static tests are a substitute for native Windows gameplay checks of HE effects, mission completion, map rendering or the feel of repeated collisions. The private retail ram-damage formula and matchmaking queue are not claimed to have been recovered.
