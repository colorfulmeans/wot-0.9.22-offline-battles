# v0.10.2 — Bot recovery, combat and vehicle contact fixes

**Unpublished candidate: PR verification and Windows gameplay testing are in
progress. v0.10.1 remains the latest published release.**

This update focuses on Bot movement and combat handoffs, vehicle contacts and crushing physics, map passages, battle presentation, and hidden-worker efficiency. It retains the existing route-selection and retry structure while addressing verified failures at their execution boundaries.

**Required client: Chinese HD World of Tanks 0.9.22.0.1 #1513, 32-bit.** The launcher and bundled server require 64-bit Windows.

## Bot movement and recovery

- Fix several repeated local turning, forward/reverse and temporary-bypass cycles. Completed escape movements and bypass endpoints now return control without automatically regenerating the same action.
- Align local movement and turn previews with actual driving sweeps, including signed turns across ±180 degrees, while retaining real collision checks.
- Preserve meaningful progress across local actions. Revisited points, small oscillations and repeated alignment must not indefinitely renew progress timers.
- Recognize effective wreck pushing in upper-level progress accounting. Unproductive pushes can terminate and return to existing retries and replanning.
- Narrow advisory wreck avoidance to permit closer rubbing and sliding. Physical collision bodies remain full size.
- Fix some side-contact reverse vetoes and coarse-grid refusals of exits that native checks can prove usable.
- Complete the handoff from final route arrival to capture, parking and combat behavior.

## Combat, aiming and dispatch

- Preserve target identity and shooting evidence across the planner, movement adapter and firing checks, reducing interrupted aiming and unnecessary target changes.
- Resolve conflicts between stationary engagement, mechanical gun laying, blocked-shot approach and bounded firing-position relocation.
- Prioritize engagement during ordinary movement and recovery. Base-defense travel retains movement priority; defenders prioritize active capturers while preserving existing attacker priority.
- Return unengaged turrets to the chassis bearing, including on banked ground.
- Improve close-contact aiming and firing handoffs without bypassing gun limits, terrain, wrecks or friendly obstruction.
- Order aim candidates as lower hull, hull center, hull left, hull right, upper hull, upper turret, turret left, turret right and turret center. Retain a usable current candidate and check mechanical elevation feasibility.
- Remove low-health automatic withdrawal. Retain empty-magazine withdrawal for long reloads and improve committed magazine follow-ups.
- Evaluate pressure-based lane redistribution once per minute and align its assignment leases and return lifecycle.
- Exclude vehicles actively contributing to enemy-base capture from base-defense selection.

## Maps, passages and destructible objects

- Treat locomotives and railway wagons as solid obstacles consistently across baked navigation and runtime checks. Repair some train-overlapping spawn positions.
- Correct destructible catalogs and native identities, including Lakeville props, and improve multi-part building crushing.
- Retain the original Himmelsdorf and Winter Himmelsdorf base grids, adding local fine meshes at narrow passages. Synchronize their passage data and restore shipping default routes.
- Connect editor grid visualization and route inspection to the navigation data actually used by Bots.
- Refine Windstorm bridge decks, both approaches and surveyed shallow-bank bypasses locally, with shared admission across pathfinding, local exits and driving.
- Align navigation and driving wading protection at 1.2 m, retaining real collision, deep-water and fall protection.
- Detect disconnected local height layers, such as bridge decks above vehicles, without introducing a new slope veto.
- Adopt the revised El Halluf light-tank route as the default.

## Support, landing and crushing

- Keep the five-point support rule: enable detailed support when at least one point is invalid; return to simplified support when all five are valid.
- Align support selection stages, reference heights, grounding and contact settlement, addressing several bridge-edge rollback and repeated-switching failures.
- Improve vehicle-roof support and vertical contact classification to reduce abnormal separation after landing on another vehicle.
- Fix missing or duplicate initial crush impacts and inconsistencies between admitted support contacts and continuing crush damage.
- While both vehicles are alive, initial impacts and continuing pressure use collision damage. Pressure from a dead upper vehicle becomes environmental damage rather than the former driver's credited damage. A living upper vehicle does not take continuing pressure damage solely from resting on a dead lower vehicle.
- Fix passive player-wreck updates and stale wreck poses, and reconcile some post-contact position, speed and grounding mismatches.

## Battle presentation, results and replays

- Restore stock battle-ribbon callbacks; fix some stuck collision and spotting notifications.
- Correct missing module-state updates and stale damage states reappearing after repair.
- Prevent some postmortem fire-state reactivation.
- Preserve valid spotting updates during spectator mode so visible enemies are not lost with an entire discarded batch.
- Restore the native bonds section and complete all-Tier-X battle reward accounting.
- Fix replay recording issues and extend the bounded recording stream capacity.

## Performance and verification

- Reuse exact synchronous collision and ground queries and prepared geometry where valid, reducing repeated sampling and matrix construction.
- Continue native migration of suitable spatial-scan and preparation work; verify the shipping native module against its source inputs.
- Expand stage timings and distinguish actual task age from queue/request waiting time.
- Preserve diagnostic causes through cache reuse, early returns, action completion and final execution, with separate current and historical evidence times.
- Update obsolete tests and expand regression coverage for current recovery, combat, passage and contact contracts.

- Remove a diagnostic callback reference cycle that accumulated unreachable objects during repeated updates.
- Correct stale test inputs, CI command folding and the replay-reader package integrity pin; retain strict regression and package checks.

## Launch entries

### Normal launch

**WoT 0.9.22 Offline Battles** (`wot-0.9.22-offline-battles.exe`): normal play, custom lineups and ordinary battle testing.

### Three optional test entries

- **START_SPIKE_PROFILE.bat — Performance diagnostics:** records detailed hidden-worker timings for collision, ground sampling, navigation and other stages. Export an error report after the test to collect the diagnostics.
- **START_ROOF_CRUSH_TEST.bat — Bridge push/drop test:** uses Victory Gate. The player starts on the bridge, six friendly Type 59s park broadside along its edge, remaining friendlies wait at the bridgehead, and enemy Type 59s wait below. Bots hold position and do not fire. Push vehicles off the bridge to test landing, roof support, impacts and sustained crushing.
- **START_ROOF_SHOT_DROP_TEST.bat — Shot-triggered drop test:** uses Victory Gate. The player starts below the bridge; fifteen enemy Type 59s wait above. Each successful player shot releases one Bot, allowing repeatable tests of impacts onto the player, sustained pressure and wreck crushing. Bots do not fire.

Fully exit both the game and launcher before switching entries.

## Remaining observations

Heavy congestion, narrow passages and multiple wrecks can still prevent a vehicle from escaping; successful recovery is not guaranteed. Some low vegetation has an unnatural crushed pose. Its current destructibility is retained to prioritize passage and playability.
