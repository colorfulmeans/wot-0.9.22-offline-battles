# v1.1.0 — Native missions, rebuilt notifications and a new vehicle viewer

This release updates the offline battles launcher and mod for the **Chinese HD World of Tanks 0.9.22.0.1 client, build #1513**. It includes the repairs and additions completed since v1.0.0.

## Missions and vehicle rewards

- Rebuild ordinary daily missions using the original client's mission list, progress and post-battle presentation. Six small daily objectives are drawn from the compatible offline mission pool.
- **Add strategic vehicle-reward missions.** These are new objectives, separate from the personal campaigns for vehicles such as the StuG IV and Object 260.
- Arrange strategic objectives by difficulty, with daily, weekly and monthly objectives in each row:

| Difficulty | Minimum base XP in each winning battle | Reward vehicle pool |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Simple | 1,000 | Tiers II–IV |
| Normal | 1,500 | Tiers V–VII |
| Hard | 2,000 | Tiers VIII–IX |
| Elite | 2,500 | Tier X |

| Objective period | Qualifying wins required | Vehicle reward |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Daily | 5 | 3-day rental |
| Weekly | 25 | 30-day rental |
| Monthly | 100 | Permanent vehicle |

- Count qualifying battles independently for each objective. One battle can advance multiple objectives; several low-XP battles cannot be added together to meet a single-battle XP threshold.
- Reset daily objectives at 08:00 Beijing time, weekly objectives on Monday at 08:00 and monthly objectives on the first day at 08:00. Weekly and monthly reward vehicles retain their own period identities instead of changing at the daily reset.
- Draw vehicle rewards using per-save random state. Prefer vehicles not permanently owned and not already rented; if all remaining non-permanent vehicles are rented, extend an existing rental. If the entire eligible pool is permanently owned, award credits instead.
- Convert a rental to permanent ownership when a permanent reward or launcher addition grants that vehicle. Use the reviewed offline valuation overrides for placeholder prices; modern live-server prices are not substituted.

## First-win bonuses

- Apply **x2 on weekdays and x5 on Saturdays and Sundays** to each vehicle's first win of the game day.
- Use the same Beijing-time 08:00 day boundary for availability, garage markers and settlement. Mission base-XP requirements exclude first-win and other reward multipliers.

## Rebuilt native system notifications

- Rebuild notification delivery around the client's native Received, Removed and information templates, with localized text and native item presentation.
- Cover launcher changes to credits, gold, free XP, bonds, premium-account days, income multipliers, vehicles, garage slots, appearance inventory, crew, barracks capacity and Personal Reserves.
- Report personal-campaign grants and withdrawals, ordinary and strategic mission rewards, rental grants, extensions, expiry and credit compensation.
- Keep pending notifications in durable queues. An individual failed entry can be retried without preventing other entries from being delivered.
- Preserve personal-campaign rules and battle bond reward calculations.

## Battle and Bot repairs

- Correct spotting relay, assist attribution and information expiry. A destroyed observer retains already delivered information until that information expires.
- Repair wreck turret and gun poses, including rotated Bot wrecks and discrepancies between rendered and diagnostic angles.
- Allow pushed wrecks to break eligible destructible obstacles, and improve contact-force propagation through combinations of live vehicles and wrecks.
- Prevent false airborne descent when a temporary support datum disappears across a small, verified ground gap, addressing the captured Lakeville T-50 failure. Roof, large-drop and slope guards remain in effect.
- Suppress whole-model felling for the reviewed low-health grass and bush resources, preventing connected vegetation patches from being thrown into the air. Real trees retain their destruction behavior.
- Change initial main-road preference from v1.0.0's **three vehicles of any class per physical road** to **three vehicles of the same class per team and physical road**. Total occupancy still helps balance allocations; this is a preference threshold, not an absolute capacity limit. Artillery allocation remains separate.
- Repair Bot equipment priorities and legal fallbacks, sparse exact-lineup handling and room-transition persistence.
- Use the current, manually reviewed default routes as the navigation test baseline.

## Launcher and save editing

- Unify action-button styles, equal-width action groups, spacing, alignment and section titles inside their borders throughout the launcher and nested editors.
- Add consistent nation, vehicle-type and tier filters. Show internal vehicle identities where duplicate display names would otherwise be ambiguous, and remove forced IS defaults while preserving valid remembered selections.
- Add premium-account day editing, Personal Reserves inventory editing and configurable garage-slot additions.
- Separate account saving from medal-history editing. Improve Chinese validation errors and left-align status/help text.
- Split appearance inventory into **My styles** and **Preset styles**, with quantity-based additions, vehicle search and compatibility filtering. Correct binding, rental-unit and install/refund handling.
- Seed a fresh default or newly created fully unlocked save with 1,000 units of the supported individual appearance items. Preset-style quantities retain their existing rules; existing named saves are not automatically refilled.

## Armor and internal-module viewer

- Replace dense wireframe presentation with filled, softly lit armor surfaces and readable legends. Distinguish main armor, spaced armor and external modules.
- Add X-ray display controls for external modules, internal modules and individual crew stations, with a translucent vehicle shell for context. Show combined crew duties and distinguish duplicate crew roles.
- Identify missing or mismatched interior data explicitly rather than inventing module positions. Community-supplied geometry is not silently installed as authoritative battle data.
- Add turret and gun preview selection with compatibility matching. Keep the counterpart when it is valid; otherwise select the first compatible item in the client's native order.
- Add group/item visibility, opacity controls and a display-settings reset separate from camera reset.
- Introduce GPU rendering with resident geometry, depth-aware picking and improved transparency, plus diagnostic fallback reporting. Raise the default camera to a 20-degree view from above.
- Preview configuration changes do not save vehicle attribute modifications unless explicitly saved.

## Unsupported entries and diagnostics

- Lock unsupported battle-mode and server-reticle settings, Premium Shop, Hotspot, Training Center, Stronghold and disconnect entry points, including room transitions.
- Keep the gold purchase display in its normal color while blocking navigation to the retired payment website.
- Remove the persistent highlight only from the unsupported battle-campaign setting.
- Improve build/session identity, battle diagnostics, error reports and verification coverage; correct related career-stat presentation.

## Updating

1. Close the game and old launcher, then extract the complete new package into a new folder.
2. Start the new launcher and select the supported game directory. For LAN battles, update and restart every participant.
3. Edit saves while the game is closed. Pending changes are reported through system messages after entering the garage.

The rebuilt ordinary and strategic mission counters start under the new rules; historical battles are not converted into progress. Existing assets and rewards already granted are retained. The default fully unlocked seed is replaced once by the new seed; existing named saves are preserved. **Personal-campaign progress and rules are not reset by this mission rebuild.**

## Verification and limits

The Windows x64 launcher and server are rebuilt from the release commit. Publication checks inspect the outer archive, executable versions, embedded client archive and mod, native architectures, frozen launcher source equality and packaged editor startup. Verification records are maintained in `COMPATIBILITY_REVIEW.md`.

This release targets the exact client listed above. Interior coverage varies by vehicle. The fixes do not imply that every map, navigation scenario or native-client issue has been resolved.
