# 1.0.0 Release Notes

1.0.0 introduces the save-based crew editor, expanded Bot configuration and class selection, route/defense editing, artillery sorties, empty-ammunition behavior and dynamic five-point support. It adds fall/fire module damage and improves aiming, navigation, replay playback, audio and maintenance.

## 1. Supported client and updates

- Supports Chinese HD World of Tanks **0.9.22.0.1 #1513**, with the 32-bit game. The launcher and bundled server require 64-bit Windows.
- Extract the complete launcher package, keeping `_internal`, licenses and companion files together. The launcher installs its matching mod.
- Ordinary updates do not require save initialization or factory reset. Versions share offline data and retain existing independent profiles.
- LAN participants should update together and restart the room/server/game.

## 2. Launcher and entrances

- Fix isolated-server imports, packaged module inclusion and cold startup. Strengthen native/resource/package identity checks.
- Closing a single-player game also closes its hidden simulation worker and temporary server, leaving the launcher open. Explicit LAN rooms remain under Stop room/launcher-exit control.
- Normal EXE logging remains compact. **START_DIAGNOSTIC_TEST.bat** enables detailed gameplay evidence; **START_SPIKE_PROFILE.bat** enables performance profiling.
- **START_ROOF_CRUSH_TEST.bat** is the Mountain Pass **bridge push/drop test**: start above and push parked friendly vehicles off to test landing, crushing and wreck contact.
- **START_ROOF_SHOT_DROP_TEST.bat** is the **shot-triggered drop test**: start below; each successful shot releases a Bot above for repeatable roof impacts.
- Fixtures remain separate from saved normal lineups. Detailed-log budgets suppress repeated construction/encoding while retaining errors and lifecycle messages.

## 3. Vehicle editor and languages

- Complete Chinese/English numeric ranges and invalid-input messages, including projectiles per trigger.
- Open editors follow the main language choice while preserving filters, selected vehicle/field, preview position and unsaved input; switching does not save or reload drafts.
- Retain validation/native units, clarify magazine/trigger intervals, enforce trigger/magazine limits and fire only real remaining rounds on partial triggers.
- Restore generic ammunition controls after fixed-SPG previews and use native shell-speed comparison colours.

## 4. Bot parameters, fittings and ammunition

- Add independent development, equipment, ammunition ratios, consumables, crew and outfit overrides. Inherit each field from global through team/class to specific slots; explicit launcher settings retain priority.
- Use legal effective vehicle/gun fittings. Stock development permits no equipment. Improve profile import/name handling, drafts, application and independent undo histories. Default is read-only.
- Offer four equipment modes—none, one device, three standard devices, or three improved devices with matching directive—alongside Rookie, Regular, Veteran and Elite tiers. Development supports Stock, Intermediate and Fully developed, with separate crew/consumable choices.
- Rebuild effective-value previews around final inherited values, replacing old example-only text.
- Fix delayed next-shot switching and single-round magazine backup handoff. Allocate magazine ratios as whole magazines; all-zero ratios carry no ammunition.
- Add a total initial-stock limit without enlarging native capacity/magazines or changing player ammunition. Fix repeated initial magazine state writeback.
- Distinguish exhausted stock from reloading, an empty magazine or one exhausted type.

### Bot shell switching

- Decisions use actual shot results and stock. Zero-weight manual types receive no allocation.
- Ordinary guns try the next available policy stage after two zero-HP hits. Positive damage resets the count; pure misses are not zero-damage hits.
- HE-primary guns try their designated non-HE backup after two direct non-penetrating hits; splash is separate.
- SPGs try stocked **enhanced HE** after two shots with neither hit nor damage. Hits or damaging splash reset the count.
- Multi-projectile triggers aggregate actually launched groups; one failed group reaches the threshold.
- Changing targets or moving over three horizontal metres resets context; rotation/small local movement does not continually reset it. Late old-context outcomes cannot pollute a new decision.
- Magazine guns return after a whole backup magazine unless further escalation is pending. Single-shot HE-primary guns return after one probe; single-shot SPGs and ordinary HE stages return after two shots. One good non-HE backup shot does not alone force a return.
- Switching invents no ammunition and restarts no reload. Already launched shells retain their frozen identity and settlement.

## 5. Waiting room, class selection and lineups

- Rebuild the room with native training-room resources. Improve fonts, colours, sizes, map/roster controls, binding and initialization. Remember settings across refreshes.
- Teams choose Bot classes independently. Random shows all five checked but disabled and remembers manual choices.
- Manual checks guarantee one **Bot** of each checked class and forbid unchecked automatic types. Humans do not satisfy guarantees; fixed launcher Bots can and are retained.
- When capacity shrinks, cancel newest checks first and explain them. Growing does not restore cancelled choices. All manual choices empty returns to Random.
- Manual SPG selection guarantees one even in small teams. Random ranges remain 0 at sizes 1–6, 0–1 at 7–9, 0–2 at 10–12 and 0–3 at 13–15.
- Refuse impossible tier/class/quota configurations with a system reason instead of substituting forbidden types. Synchronize normalized choices after refusal.

## 6. Saves, crew and barracks

### Save-based crew editor

- Recruit at **Saves → Customize save → Members**, targeting the selected save's barracks.
- Select nation, class, real vehicle, actual seat/profession, gender, training and skill/perk count.
- Include combined duties in skill limits, count shared skills once and number duplicate seats separately.
- Offer 50/75/100% training. Choose actual secondary skills in the client at 100%; lower training cannot receive invalid ordinary-skill credits.
- Add with the game closed, deliver next startup and show a system notice. Persist save-bound requests and retry identity to prevent loss or duplicate delivery after interruption.
- Preserve pending requests when full and include them and skill entitlements in backup/restore.
- Correct female identity data; zero-XP Brothers in Arms counts in the selected total. Skill credits belong to the member and do not renew after moving seats or restarting.

### Capacity, filtering and defaults

- Add **Add 16 barracks berths**, increasing by sixteen per click. Recruitment checks occupied and pending space before accepting, with receiving-side protection.
- Filter crew vehicle choices through valid standard/model rules, removing invalid retired entries. Use full nation names and native garage nation order in Members and Garage tanks.
- Add **Initialize save** with confirmation. Align save actions in two rows of four and prevent legacy imports reviving reset data.
- Fully unlocked defaults use **full-skill crew, three legal improved devices and matching directives with auto-resupply**. Saved player changes are not repeatedly overwritten; career saves keep their own rules.
- Edit compatible style inventory, including hidden styles, copies and missing stock. Rental copies keep original counts; later use/sales are not refilled after first unlocked initialization.
- Add independent Bot outfit inheritance/on/off, respecting locked outfits.

- Symmetric maps with spawns inside bases retain both spawn endpoints. Asymmetric offset-spawn routes target the enemy capture circle. SPG attacks always run from home base to enemy base.
- Symmetry reverses intermediate paths while retaining team endpoints/home defense; disable it on unsupported maps. Clear wrong-team lines and stale drag/wait state on team changes.
- Draw primaries solid and reserves dashed in class colours. Fix primary/reserve controls in the overview; disable only the already active state.

Waiting/parking/endpoints cannot be junctions; junctions cannot become wait points. SPG attacks do not gain ordinary defense branches.

## 8. Route allocation and arrival

## 9. Recovery, return cycles and narrow passages

- Retain local avoidance, pushing, reversing and turning. Failure from B towards C tries D; another failure returns through B/A and the old route to its start.
- Return remains recovery and skips waits. Finish transfer only after reaching the new start and beginning the new route. Failed cycles may retry without permanent exhausted-attempt stops.
- Combat pauses recovery timing and preserves progress; reconnect from actual position after combat movement. Fix stale control/task ownership after local completion or failed return/switch connections.
- Improve general gate/bridge narrow-passage guidance, align preview and real rotation/forward/reverse directions, including ±180-degree transitions.
- Fix unnecessary reverse refusal beside wrecks and coarse costs hiding proved clear exits. Count useful pushed-object displacement; hand unproductive pushing back to retries.
- Keep actual hull/wreck volumes while allowing legal edge sliding. Unify navigation/steering/recovery arena boundaries.
- Release consumed old steering tips before processing new navigation targets, preventing false arrival far from the objective while retaining unfinished legal alignment.

## 10. Redistribution, base defense and player orders

- Priority is automatic defense → combat → recovery → redistribution → player commands → ordinary travel. Preserve aiming, ballistics, firing and collision rules.
- Combat invalidates player orders; they do not revive afterwards. Defense prioritizes hull travel with opportunistic turret fire; blocked tasks retain recovery.
- Evaluate redistribution every 180 seconds. Reverse to the old start before changing road. Expiry retains a completed transfer or reconnects to the old passed point.
- Exclude active recovery/transfers, actual capture and empty-stock capture from repeated dispatch; distinguish reservations from arrivals and retain physical road occupancy.
- Reverse defense skips waits, uses a reachable branch, or continues to the start/base after passing its junction. Repeated failure retains checked point-skipping/direct-base fallback.
- Automatic defense overrides player requests and releases after thirty continuous seconds without enemy occupiers, including zero-point enemies inside.
- Player guard has ninety seconds from issue; first base entry switches once to thirty continuous safe seconds.
- Inside base, legally shootable high-point occupiers outrank old outside targets; ties use normal scoring. Restore normal targeting afterwards.
- Clean defense fallback state; ordinary vehicles rejoin their road and SPGs resume the interrupted parking/sortie identity.
- Share a ten-second cooldown across an issuer's command types, starting only after real acceptance. Only recipients acknowledge; eligible responders reject once during cooldown. Rejected commands are not queued. No recipient means no cooldown/reply.
- New legal requests replace old player requests. Attack follows the route, turn-back reaches one previous point, base return reverses, attention/stop/follow reconnect after expiry. Follow positions track the player and end on death/departure/expiry.

## 11. Capture protection and empty ammunition

- Remove extra three-capturer assignment and outer capture-guard tasks.
- **Entering the enemy base circle completes terminal arrival and starts capture; no pursuit of the original last waypoint.**
- More than three vehicles may stay inside; native capture-speed contribution limits do not restrict entry.
- Protect capture from unnecessary chase, reload adjustment and redistribution destinations outside the circle. Re-enter after shoves and complete arrival/stop/combat handoff without rebuilding a finished goal.
- Require known valid zero stock for all shell types. Unknown data, reloading, an empty magazine or one empty type do not qualify.
- On first activation retire old combat/player/redistribution/defense/parking and paused-recovery ownership once.
- Travel to enemy capture, skip waits and do not restart stationary combat when hit. Opportunistic turret aiming retains checks; no rounds are invented and the last launched shot settles normally.
- Exclude empty vehicles from external calls but count occupancy. Retain their own recovery, skipping, reverse return and switching. Stay capturing/re-enter and clear state next round.

## 12. Artillery deployment and sorties

- Separate parking/automatic deployment, endgame sortie and empty sortie identities.
- Reserve at most three parking slots, one per position including travel. Replace dead owners from free SPGs; failed replacement disables that position for the round.
- Unassigned SPGs automatically navigate/deploy/fire without ordinary-road capacity. Deployment, relocation and parking return use automatic navigation; obsolete fixed links do not return through import/reset.
- With only SPG Bots surviving, retain up to three armed sortie participants; humans are excluded from that eligibility test. Prefer free SPGs, then randomly choose parked ones.
- Home travel, combat and recovery keep slots. Fill real vacancies, stop parking replacement and let others wait/fire.
- Return home before the independent red dashed attack road. Keep SPG ballistics, pitch/yaw, reload and firing checks; old parking is not required to engage.
- Automatic defense retains sortie identity and resumes attack afterwards.
- Apply C/D/B/A/home recovery. Borrow valid less occupied ordinary primary/reserve roads, randomize ties, avoid failed roads when possible and retry them if necessary.
- Borrowing uses a reversed defense connector from base, then the ordinary road; without a connector join its start. Missing SPG attack data tries ordinary roads before final automatic fallback.
- Empty SPGs bypass Bot-only-endgame and three-armed-sortie admission. Return home first, skip parking/waits and prefer the SPG road; after three users borrow low-occupancy roads.
- Count reservations before arrival. Retain own recovery but reject external defense/player/redistribution calls. Fix stale target-directed hull turning while retaining turret aiming.
